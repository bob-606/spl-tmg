# SPL with TMG Privileges only
*An edge case based on the [EASA Part‑SFCL](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/sailplane-rule-book-easy-access-rules).*

---

## Intro: Is This Even Possible?🤔

Under the [EASA Part‑SFCL](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/sailplane-rule-book-easy-access-rules), a **Touring Motor Glider (TMG)** is legally **treated as a sailplane**.

> *"When requirements of Part-SFCL refer to 'sailplanes', this includes powered sailplanes as well as touring motor gliders (TMGs), unless otherwise specified."*  
> — **GM1 SFCL.001 Scope**

Thus, a student may complete the entire SPL training (theories, flights, and skill test) on a TMG.

For example:
> *"Applicants shall complete the skill test in... or a TMG..."*  
> — **SFCL.145(b)**

However, there is a critical limitation:

> *"If the skill test as specified in point SFCL.145 has been completed in a TMG, the privileges of the SPL shall be limited to TMGs."*  
> — **SFCL.150(d)**

This doc is about the requirements to obtain a **『 TMG‑limited SPL 』**, based strictly on the [EASA Part‑SFCL](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/sailplane-rule-book-easy-access-rules).

---

## 1. Entry Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Reference |
|-------------|--------|-----------|
| Minimum age | **16 years** at licence issue (solo flights permitted from age 14) | SFCL.120, SFCL.125 |
| Medical certificate | Valid **Part‑MED Class 2** or **LAPL medical certificate** | SFCL.115(c) |
| Training organisation | Complete the course at an **ATO** or a **DTO** | SFCL.130(a) |

---

## 2. Theoretical Knowledge (SFCL.135)

You must pass the CAA exam(s) in **9 subjects**, taken **before** the skill test (SFCL.145(c)) and under the authority of a single Member State's competent authority.

**Examination rules (AMC1 SFCL.135):**
- An ATO/DTO recommendation is required and valid for **12 months**.
- Approximately **120 MCQs** in total.
- Pass mark: **75%** for each subject. 
- Must pass **all sbjects within 18 months** of the first attempt.
- Maximum **4 attempts** per subject; failure on the 4th attempt will result in a complete retake for all subjects.
- Successful cxam results are valid for **24 months** for the skill test and licence application.

---

## 3. Flight Training & Experience (SFCL.130(a)(2))

The course must be tailored to **TMG privileges**. Minimum requirements:

| Item | Minimum |
|------|---------|
| Total flight instruction (sailplanes, incl. TMG) | **15 hours** |
| …of which dual flight instruction | **10 hours** |
| …of which in **TMGs** | **6 hours** |
| …of which **dual in TMG** | **4 hours** |
| Supervised solo flight time | **2 hours** |
| **Launches / take‑offs and landings** | **45** |
| Solo cross‑country in a **TMG** | **≥ 150 km (80 NM)** + **1 full‑stop landing (@ another site)** |

---

## 4. Radio Telephony Qualification (if required by national law)

Many Member States require a **radio telephony qualification** (e.g., FCL.055 or equivalent national requirement) before exercising privileges that involve radio communication.

Check with your competent authority for the specific examination and application procedure in your State.

---

## 5. Skill Test in a TMG (SFCL.145 + AMC1)

The skill test must be taken with an **FE(S)** (Flight Examiner for Sailplanes).

Test content per **AMC1 SFCL.145(d)(2)** :

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **1. Pre‑flight operations & departure** | Documentation, flight planning, NOTAM/weather briefing, mass & balance, performance calculation, TMG inspection & servicing, engine start, taxiing, pre‑take‑off checks, take‑off, departure, ATC liaison (if applicable). |
| **2A. General airwork (powered)** | Straight & level with speed changes, climbs & climbing turns, medium (30°) turns, steep (45°) turns, slow flight, stalls (clean with power, descending‑turn approach‑to‑stall, landing configuration), descents. |
| **2B. General airwork (unpowered)** | Straight & level, medium turns, **in‑flight engine start and stop procedures**, stall in a turn. |
| **3. En‑route procedures** | Dead reckoning & map reading, maintaining altitude/heading/speed, orientation, ETAs & log keeping, diversion planning, fuel/engine management, ATC liaison. |
| **4. Approach & landing** | Arrival procedures, look‑out, short‑field & crosswind landing, flapless landing, idle‑power approach, touch‑and‑go, go‑around from low height, actions after flight. |
| **5. Abnormal & emergency procedures** | Simulated engine failure after take‑off, forced landing, precautionary landing, simulated emergencies, oral questions. |

**Some of the skill test components may be combined, at the discretion of the FE.*

**Additional notes:**
- Navigation leg must be at least **30 minutes** with ≥ 2 waypoints (may be flown separately by agreement).
- **Tolerances (guidance):** height ±150 ft; speed +15/−5 kt (take‑off/approach), ±15 kt otherwise.
- **Pass/fail:** Failure in one section → repeat only that section. Failure in two or more sections → retake the entire test. If the applicant fails within 2 attempts, further training is required before retesting.

---

## 6. Application to the Competent Authority

Submit your application to the competent authority of the Member State where you trained, using their prescribed form(s). The application typically requires:

- [ ] Copy of passport / national ID card
- [ ] Copy of valid **Part‑MED Class 2 or LAPL** medical certificate
- [ ] Radio telephony qualification (if required by the Member State)
- [ ] **Course completion certificate** from the ATO/DTO
- [ ] **Original logbooks** (or digitally signed extract for electronic logbooks)
- [ ] Copy of theoretical knowledge examination results (if not held by the authority)
- [ ] **Original SPL skill test report** (signed by the FE(S))
- [ ] Examiner designation document (as required by the authority)
- [ ] Recent passport‑style photograph
- [ ] Applicable national fee (check with your competent authority)

**Declaration:** You must confirm that you do **not** hold (or have pending) a Part‑SFCL licence in another EASA Member State and have never had one revoked or suspended there (SFCL.015(e)). False declarations may be subject to national penalties.

## 7. After Issue — Privileges & Recency

### Basic Privileges (SFCL.115)
- Act as **PIC in TMGs without remuneration** in non‑commercial operations.
- **Carriage of passengers:** Requires either:
  - ≥ **10 hours** flight time **or** **30 take‑offs/landings** as PIC **after** licence issue, **plus** one check/training flight with an FI(S); **or**
  - Hold an FI(S) certificate.
- **Remunerated / commercial operations:** Requires age **18** + **75 hours** flight time **or** **200 take‑offs/landings** as PIC after issue.

### TMG Recency — every 24 months (SFCL.160(b))
Either:
- **12 hours** flight time as PIC / dual / supervised solo, including:
  - **6 hours on TMGs**,
  - **12 take‑offs and landings**, and
  - a **≥ 1 hour training flight** with an instructor; **or**
- Pass a **proficiency check** with an FE(S) based on the TMG skill test.

### Passenger‑carrying Recency — every 90 days (SFCL.160(e)(2))
- **3 take‑offs and landings** as PIC in a TMG within the preceding 90 days.
- For carrying passengers **at night** in a TMG, at least **1** of those take‑offs/landings must be at night.

### Launching Method Currency (SFCL.155(a)(2) & (c))
- If you later obtain self‑launch privileges, you need **5 launches** (or take‑offs in a TMG) in the last **24 months** to remain current.

---

## 8. Extending to Pure Sailplanes Later (Optional, SFCL.150(e))

With a TMG‑limited SPL, you can add privileges for sailplanes **excluding TMGs** upon application, provided you:

1. Complete at an ATO/DTO the training elements of **SFCL.130(a)(2)(iv)** :
   - ≥ **7 hours** of instruction in sailplanes (excluding TMGs), including **3 hours dual**;
   - **50 km solo cross‑country** **or** **100 km dual cross‑country** (which may be completed in a TMG by derogation);
   - **plus** at least **15 launches and landings** in a non‑TMG sailplane.

2. Pass a **new skill test** in a pure sailplane, during which you must also demonstrate theoretical knowledge for sailplanes in the 5 TMG‑specific subjects (SFCL.150(e)(2)).

Training completion must be entered in your logbook and signed by the head of training of the ATO/DTO.

### 4. Solo Cross‑Country (SFCL.130(a)(2)(vi))

- **≥150 km (80 NM)** TMG flight with **1 full‑stop landing** at a different site.
- Pre‑flight: Weather, NOTAMs, route planning, ICAO FPL (if req.), mass/performance/balance checks.
- Supervised by FI(S) – pre‑flight briefing + post‑flight debrief.

---

### 5. Skill Test (AMC1 SFCL.145(d)(2))

Conducted by an **FE(S)** in a TMG.

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **1. Pre‑flight & departure** | Docs, plan, WX/NOTAM, mass/balance, perf., inspection, start, taxi, T/O, ATC |
| **2A. Powered airwork** | S&L (speed changes), climbs, 30° & 45° turns, slow flight, stalls (clean, approach, landing config) |
| **2B. Unpowered airwork** | S&L, 30° turns, in‑flight engine stop/start, stall in turn |
| **3. En‑route** | DR, map, heading/alt/speed, ETAs, diversion, fuel/engine mgmt, ATC |
| **4. Approach & landing** | Arrival, short‑field, crosswind, flapless, idle‑power, touch‑and‑go, go‑around |
| **5. Abnormal/emergency** | Engine failure after T/O, forced/precautionary landing, emergencies, oral Qs |

**Tolerances:** Height ±150 ft; speed +15/−5 kt (T/O & approach), ±15 kt otherwise.
**Pass/Fail:** Fail 1 section → retake that section; fail ≥2 → retake entire test; 2 failed attempts → further training required.

---

### 6. DTO Administration

**Course Completion:**
- Issue certificate signed by Head of Training.
- Logbook entry signed.
- Recommend for theoretical exam (valid 12 months) and skill test.
- Verify valid Part‑MED Class 2 / LAPL medical before solo.

**Records to Keep (DTO.GEN.215):**
- Training record (dates, exercises, instructor signatures).
- Logbook copies.
- Course completion certificate copy.
- Skill test report copy (FE(S) signed).
- Declaration and changes notified to competent authority.

**DTO Operational Notes:**
- **TMG definition**: Integrally mounted non‑retractable engine/propeller; capable of T/O/climb under own power.
- **FI(S) privileges**: An FI(S) *without* TMG instructional privileges may teach pure gliding exercises in a TMG, but *must* sit in the PIC seat. TMG‑specific items require an FI(S) with TMG privileges (SFCL.315(a)(4)).
- **Use of TMGs for pure sailplane training**: Max 8 hours, only pure gliding manoeuvres, if not seeking TMG privileges.
- **Extension to pure sailplanes (SFCL.150(e))** : Additional 7 hrs instruction on non‑TMG sailplanes (3 hrs dual, 15 launches/landings, XC – 50 km solo or 100 km dual, may use TMG by derogation) + new skill test in pure sailplane.
- **Post‑licence recency (TMG)** : 12 hrs (6 on TMG, 12 T/O/Ldg, 1 hr training flight) OR proficiency check within 24 months (SFCL.160(b)).
- **Passenger currency**: 3 T/O/Ldg as PIC in preceding 90 days (SFCL.160(e)(2)).

## Summary of Key Differences: DTO vs ATO

| Aspect | DTO (Appendix 1) | ATO (Appendix 2) |
|--------|------------------|------------------|
| **Status** | Declared organisation | Approved organisation |
| **Oversight** | Declaration to competent authority | Approval certificate issued by competent authority |
| **Quality system** | Not mandatory | Required (ORA.ATO.135) |
| **Operations Manual** | Not required | Required (ORA.ATO.125) |
| **Instructor standardisation** | Not formalised | Required (ORA.ATO.140) |
| **Internal audits** | Not required | Required |
| **Occurrence reporting** | Not required | Required |
| **Records** | Basic training records | Comprehensive records including quality system documentation |
